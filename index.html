Basilei,

First, thank you.

As I near my planned return to office on Monday, October 5, I want to take a moment to express my sincere gratitude for the care, concern, check-ins, gifts, prayers, and encouragement many of you and your chapters extended to me during the birth of my son and my maternity leave.

I also appreciate your doing your best to honor my time away and give me the space to be present with my family. I know there were moments when things simply called for my attention, and I did my best to resolve issues within a newborn's schedule and our bonding time. I appreciate your grace and understanding when that happened.

I am grateful to be returning knowing that our chapters and our Region continued moving forward, and I am excited about what is ahead for Western. There is a great deal happening as we begin this new biennium, and I look forward to continuing this work with you.

Thank you for leading your chapters, caring for your members and affiliates, and helping us EVOLVE the Mighty Western Region Forward, together.

Andrea Davis

Boule Update

What Changed with SPEAR & NEF

Effective Immediately

Following actions taken at Boule, members of the Sorority will no longer pay assessments to the SPEAR and NEF foundations, and there is no longer a provision for regional representatives to serve with either foundation.

The International Parliamentarian sent correspondence to the membership on Friday, September 4th, with the full details. Please review that correspondence carefully and retain it for your chapter records.

This Brief highlights the immediate change only — the official correspondence from the International Parliamentarian is the authoritative source.

Chapter Business

Don't Let Admin Become a Barrier

Please make sure your chapter is current with required reports, financial obligations, and representation requirements.

  • Some chapters have not submitted End-of-Year reports, or submitted them late — applicable ICHQ fines have been or will be assessed.
  • Some chapters submitted their 990s late.
  • Chapters without representation at Boule for two consecutive years receive a fine from ICHQ.
  • Chapters without representation at Regional Conference for two consecutive years receive a fine from the Western Region.
Outstanding reports, fines, or compliance issues may affect a chapter's ability to conduct intake or seat delegates at the upcoming conference. If your chapter has an open item, now is the time to resolve it.
